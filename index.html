Iowa Carbon Pipelines

This map helps users locate Iowa counties impacted by planned carbon pipeline construction, ordinances, and litigation. Basic instructions are as follows:

  1. Use the "Map layers" control in the upper right corner of the map to switch to the layer showing which counties have enacted pipeline-related ordinances or litigation, or have news reported about pipeline proposals.
  2. You can also use this control to filter the planned pipelines by company (Navigator, Summit, and Wolf). Pipelines are drawn as a solid line (Navigator), a dashed line (Summit), and a dotted line (Wolf).
  3. With the ordinance layer on, you can click or press Enter on counties with ordinances on the map to open ordinance-related documents in new web browser tabs. When more than one ordinance exists, you may need to set your browser preferences to allow popups for this page.
  4. You can also search for county ordinances, litigation, or news using the county menu in the sidebar. The results appear at the bottom of the sidebar. This is the quickest option for keyboard and screen reader users.
  5. You can collapse this sidebar with the "Close sidebar" button at its upper right corner.
  6. Keyboard users: press Tab to move between controls. When the map has focus, use the arrow keys to pan and the plus and minus keys to zoom.
